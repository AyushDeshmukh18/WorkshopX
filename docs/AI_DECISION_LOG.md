# AI Decision Log

This log records all key architectural and product decisions, trade-offs, and explicit rejections throughout the development of **FirstBuild Engine**.

Format:
`date | I asked | AI suggested | decision & why | what changed`

---

| Date | User / Requirement Asked | Alternative / Considered Option | Decision & Rationale | What Changed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2026-10-02 | WhatsApp automated student outreach & registration confirmations | Paid WhatsApp Business API / bulk messaging service | **REJECTED.** Cost is prohibitive for a zero-budget campaign; high spam detection risk and Meta template policy blocks. **Chosen:** Campus Captain forward kits, copy-to-clipboard WhatsApp message templates, and native `wa.me/?text=` viral share links. | Implemented captain forwarding kit and client-side `wa.me` share buttons. Zero messaging cost. |
| 2026-10-02 | Building lead list of college students and placement coordinators | Scraping student phone numbers from LinkedIn, college directories, or public PDFs | **REJECTED.** Serious violation of privacy ethics and India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. High reputation risk for college relationships. **Chosen:** Explicit opt-in consent checkboxes on sign-up; outreach contacts table requiring documented `consent_basis`. | Added mandatory `consent_basis` column in outreach contacts and explicit consent at registration. |
| 2026-10-02 | Urgency generation on registration page | Fake countdown timers, synthetic "3 seats remaining" tickers, or artificial FOMO tickers | **REJECTED.** Destroys student trust; easily debunked in developer tools; unprofessional. **Chosen:** Honest, real-time seat tracking calculated strictly as `registration_cap - count(verified_registrations)`. When full, waitlist opens cleanly. | Real-time seat counter bound to Postgres verified count; no client-side spoofing. |
| 2026-10-02 | Student acquisition budget allocation | Broad paid social ad campaigns (Meta / Google Ads) | **REJECTED.** ₹500 buys only ~15 to 25 low-intent registrations with dismal workshop attendance. **Chosen:** Zero-cost Campus Captain network (40 club heads/CRs) + student referral loop with milestone rewards (priority access at 3 refs, verified captain certificate at 10 refs). | Capital preserved for high-intent grassroots distribution and captain enablement. |
| 2026-10-02 | Notification and reminder workflow orchestration | Self-hosted or cloud n8n automation workflows | **REJECTED.** Self-hosted n8n introduces tunnel/hosting failure points, webhook fragility, and maintenance overhead. **Chosen:** Built-in Postgres transactional outbox pattern with an idempotent `/api/cron/tick` endpoint triggered by GitHub Actions and redundant external cron. | All automation runs in-engine with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` atomic guarantees. |
| 2026-10-02 | System modularity and authentication structure | Microservices architecture, self-hosted LLM fine-tuning, complex OAuth providers | **REJECTED.** Over-engineering for a 30-hour build and 500-seat workshop. **Chosen:** Clean Next.js modular monolith with serverless edge routes, zero-password email OTP, and signed HTTP-only JWT session cookies via `jose`. | Fast iteration, unified deployment on Vercel Hobby, zero operational friction. |
| 2026-10-02 | Workshop reminder scheduling engine | Vercel Hobby built-in cron triggers | **REJECTED.** Vercel Hobby plan only permits once-daily cron execution at imprecise intervals, making T-24h, T-2h, and T-15m precision reminders impossible. **Chosen:** GitHub Actions workflow scheduled every 5 minutes (`*/5 * * * *`) calling `/api/cron/tick`, backed by cron-job.org redundancy. | Time-critical reminders dispatch within +/-5 minutes of target milestone windows. |
| 2026-10-02 | UI visual identity and aesthetics | Trendy "vibe-coded" design with purple glow gradients, pill-shaped buttons, emoji headings, and over-the-top animations | **REJECTED.** Appears unprofessional, distracting, and slows mobile performance on 3G networks. **Chosen:** Crisp, high-contrast engineering aesthetic with sharp borders, professional slate/emerald color system, Lucide icons, and zero layout shift. | High readability, fast rendering on low-tier mobile devices, trustworthy enterprise feel. |
